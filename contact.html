Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Our Products

How are WPC fluted panels installed on bare or painted walls?

WPC panels feature an engineered tongue-and-groove interlocking system. They can be mounted directly onto flat plastered or gypsum walls using architectural silicone adhesive and discrete starter clips. No heavy framing or wet masonry work is needed.

Are the PU Stone sheets real rock or lightweight?

Our PU Stone sheets are cast from high-density structural polyurethane using real mountain rock molds. They capture genuine 3D crags, veins, and tactile rock depth, yet weigh only 2.5kg per sheet! This makes them easy to mount on high walls and drywall without structural load concerns.

Do you ship to Lahore, Islamabad, and other cities in Pakistan?

Yes! We supply nationwide on a daily basis. Bulk and retail orders are professionally palletized and dispatched via trusted goods transport networks with door-to-door delivery options.
