Direct Importer Advantage

Eliminating Middlemen to Bring World-Class Materials to Pakistan

Traditional wall renovations in Pakistan relied on heavy natural stone masonry, prone to crumbling, and expensive carpentry requiring endless painting, sanding, and termite treatments.

Multi Decor revolutionized this by directly importing high-density eco-polymer WPC Fluted Louvers and ultra-light PU Mountain Rock Slabs that deliver unmatched luxury aesthetics while being 100% waterproof, termite-immune, and installed in hours.
